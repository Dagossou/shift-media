The people behind the lens.

A video, photography and live streaming studio based in Lomé, Togo.

Our mission

We capture people, purpose and possibility. We help churches, NGOs, institutions and brands across West Africa tell their stories with care, through interviews, events, reportage and live broadcasts that move people to act.

Our vision

To become West Africa’s most trusted visual storytelling partner, where every story told well inspires change, and where a new generation of African creatives grows its craft.
